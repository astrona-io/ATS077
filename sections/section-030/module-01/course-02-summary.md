# Summary

Application environments provide different contexts for development, verification and production operation. Their purposes determine data, identity, capacity and access boundaries. Reusing known artifacts and compatible configuration contracts reduces variation, but test conditions do not automatically reproduce production traffic or dependencies. Isolation should be explicit rather than inferred from a folder or namespace name, and temporary environments need a deliberate lifecycle for resources and data.
