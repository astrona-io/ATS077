# How This Course Is Made

Authors map sections to the official Certified Cloud Native Platform Engineering Associate (CNPA) objectives and check mechanisms against primary documentation. They review examples for responsible components, prerequisites and meaningful failure cases.

Structural checks verify reading order, section introductions, closing summaries, draft metadata and YAML question contracts. Labs and playgrounds are checked statically (`astrona validate`, shell syntax checks and YAML parsing). They have not been run yet, so they are marked untested until `astrona test` passes.

The [contributors](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS077/graphs/contributors) maintain the material. To [report a mistake](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS077/issues), identify the page and disputed statement. The [license](https://github.com/astrona-io/ATS077/blob/main/LICENSE) governs reuse.

Objectives belong to their respective owners. Teaching and knowledge checks are original practice material. Application names are illustrative and have no special technical meaning.
