# Welcome To The Course

This course develops the official Platform Engineering Core Fundamentals objectives for Certified Cloud Native Platform Engineering Associate (CNPA). The certification uses a multiple-choice examination. The course is independently authored material with hands-on labs and does not claim to simulate the examination.

Each section starts with its own introduction and learning outcomes. Each module provides goals, developed teaching and a final summary. Lesson counts follow the subject rather than a fixed number.

YAML knowledge checks are registered separately for future presentation. They follow the relevant summaries and include original questions and answer feedback. Technical pages remain draft for review.

Read the explanations and worked examples to identify the responsible component, intended operation and limits of each claim. Each module also has a playground and a graded lab on a local kind cluster, and each section ends with a capstone lab and a quiz.
