# Summary

GitOps combines declarative versioned intent, automatic retrieval and continuous reconciliation. Drift correction depends on controller policy and permissions. Configuration history supports review, but recovery may also need application data handling.

Helm uses charts, templates and values; Kustomize composes resources through bases and overlays. Both need review of the rendered manifests. Rendering, applying, reconciling and validating application behavior are separate responsibilities.
