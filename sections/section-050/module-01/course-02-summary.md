# Summary

Platform goals should connect capabilities to useful outcomes for internal users. Golden paths provide supported ways to complete recurring tasks, while explicit exceptions accommodate needs outside their scope. Incremental adoption and user feedback test whether those paths reduce avoidable work. Feature counts and mandated usage do not prove value. The platform needs ownership, support and measurement so its promises remain meaningful as users and dependencies change.
